<Title>Typography</Title>

**Font:** Urbanist, one family for text and figures. The app loads it and sets `--font-urbanist`
(`FONT_FAMILY_VARIABLE`); without it the stack falls back to `"Urbanist"`, `"Segoe UI"`, `system-ui`.

**Weights:** 400 regular, 500 medium, 600 semibold, 700 bold (`theme.typography.fontWeight*`). Each variant has a
default weight; a component sets another one from the theme where the design asks for it.

**Numerals:** tabular on the whole page (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on `body`).

Sizes and line heights are in `rem` (px / 16). On a narrow screen the net amount switches from `display` to
`displayNarrow`; the component does that, not the variant.
